ABOUT ME

About me

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Physics & Astronomy at the University of Delaware.

I completed my bachelor's degree in Physics at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia before moving to Delaware to begin my Ph.D., where I work with Prof. Sally Dodson-Robinson.

My research focuses on stellar variability and its impact on the detection and characterization of exoplanets. I am particularly interested in understanding the timescales over which stellar signals remain correlated.

Beyond research, I am strongly interested in science outreach, particularly in helping Colombian students learn about and access research opportunities in astronomy around the world.
