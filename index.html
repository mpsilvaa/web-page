I look at how stars change with time.

I am a Ph.D. student in Physics & Astronomy at the University of Delaware.

My research focuses on stellar variability and the way stellar activity can complicate the search for exoplanets. I am particularly interested in understanding the timescales over which stellar signals remain correlated.

By combining astronomical observations with statistical methods, I work toward separating signals produced by stars from those that may come from orbiting planets.
